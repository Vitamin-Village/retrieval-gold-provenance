# GraphRAG-Bench Evidence-Sentence Surface Diagnostic

Generated by `census/perunit_graphragbench.py` (deterministic, offline).

## Setup

The census assigns the GraphRAG-Bench subgraph axis to bin 3 (C-hat = 0.75),
but the released units analyzed here are evidence sentences, not gold subgraphs.
This is therefore an auxiliary surface diagnostic, not a per-unit estimate of
C_subgraph. GraphRAG-Bench (medical split, 2062 questions) publishes,
per question, the KG relation(s) it was generated from (`evidence_relations`) and
sentence-level supporting evidence (`evidence`). We judge whether each evidence
sentence is a surface realization of a KG relation (derivation), and whether that
realization is a near-verbatim copy of the relation string.

## Judgment rules (deterministic, conservative)

Normalization: lowercase, punctuation -> space, whitespace collapsed.

1. **Relation realization.** An evidence sentence is `derived = 1` iff a MAJORITY
   (>= 0.50) of the content (non-stopword, length > 1) tokens of some
   `evidence_relations` string appear in the normalized sentence. Only surface
   token overlap is credited (no aliases, paraphrase, or semantic matching), so
   the derivation fraction is a conservative LOWER BOUND.
2. **Verbatim-copy.** Among matched units, a unit is verbatim iff
   the normalized sentence and its matched relation string satisfy token
   Jaccard >= 0.90, or one is a substring of the other. This describes how
   source relations are realized in text; it does not classify a subgraph unit.

## Interpretation and scope

The measured relation-realization fraction is 0.627 (question-mean) / 0.450 (sentence-pooled),
a conservative lower bound because only surface token overlap is credited (a sentence
that expresses a relation through synonyms, paraphrase, or recontextualization scores 0).
Across the three KG-native question types (Complex Reasoning, Contextual Summarize, Fact Retrieval) the per-question derivation runs 0.59-0.71;
Creative Generation is the outlier at 0.24, because its evidence sentences rewrite the
source relations into role-play / discharge-summary scenarios, diverging most in surface
form from the KG triples. This rewording is what pulls the pooled mean below the
question mean (Creative Generation contributes the most sentences).

The verbatim-copy fraction is low everywhere
(overall 0.136; per-type max 0.254), so the realized evidence is reworded rather than copied
verbatim from the KG.

These figures describe sentence surfaces only and do not establish, reject, or rank
a provenance bin for the gold subgraphs. The subgraph bin remains a methodology-level
judgment grounded in the documented ontology-to-subgraph construction and human
confirmation step, with cross-model and human dissent reported separately.
