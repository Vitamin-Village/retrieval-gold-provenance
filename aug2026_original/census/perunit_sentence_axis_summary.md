# 2Wiki Sentence-Axis Surface-Realization Diagnostic

Generated by `census/perunit_sentence_axis.py` (deterministic, offline).

## Setup

2WikiMultiHop publishes, per question, Wikidata evidence triples and sentence-level
supporting facts. We judge every supporting-fact sentence for whether it is a surface
realization of some evidence triple, then average. The census places the 2Wiki sentence
axis in bin 3 (C-hat = 0.75, KG derivation). The measured average is an auxiliary
surface statistic, not a direct reconstruction of each unit's construction lineage.

## Judgment rule (deterministic, conservative)

A sentence at (title, sent_idx) is realized (=1) iff some triple (s, r, o) satisfies:

1. norm(title) == norm(s), where norm = lowercase + whitespace collapse.
   The paren-stripped variant also accepts a match after removing one trailing
   parenthetical disambiguator from the title (e.g. "X (film)" -> "X").
2. norm(o) appears as a substring of the normalized sentence text.

No alias expansion is performed for subjects or objects, so both estimates are
conservative lower bounds: a sentence expressing the triple through an alias,
pronoun, or paraphrase of the object is scored 0.

## Interpretation relative to census bin 3 (C-hat = 0.75)

On the 400 evaluation questions the measured per-unit average is 0.812 (question-mean)
and 0.812 (sentence-pooled) under the paren-stripped rule, a conservative lower bound
because object aliases and paraphrases are not credited. Its numerical proximity to
0.75 is descriptive only: surface realization is not the construction-lineage quantity
defined by C_a. The bin-3 assignment therefore continues to rest on the documented
Wikidata-to-supporting-sentence construction, not on this statistic alone.
