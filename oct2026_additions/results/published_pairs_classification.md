# Published pairs of Appendix B.11 classified by the structure the graph/KG system retrieved over

Source rows: `analysis/scatter_circularity_gain.py` ROWS and the master table in
`aug2026_original/census/published_gains_sources.md` (row 15, GRADE, has no pair; 30 pairs remain).
Quotes were taken from the arXiv PDFs downloaded on 2026-09-30 (text extracted with PyMuPDF); page = PDF page.
Deltas are the paper's transcribed values and were not re-audited here.

Classes:
1. Gold-source KB/KG: the benchmark's own construction KB (the paper's "realistic full KB" condition).
2. System-built from the corpus (the paper's "independent" arm).
3. Induced from the benchmark's released evidence or gold paths (the paper's question-induced "aligned" arm).
4. Other pre-existing structure that is not the gold source (external KB, hyperlinks).

## Summary

**Counts.** Class 1 has 11 pairs: 4 over the STaRK SKBs from MoR and QAGNN, and 7 over the WikiMovies KB or Freebase from PullNet, GraftNet, and KV-Mem. Class 2 has 17 pairs: HippoRAG 6, HippoRAG 2 5, HopRAG 1, KG-CQR 3, and GraphRAG-Bench 2. Class 3 has none. Class 4 has 2, both from GraphRetriever.

Benchmark medians: five of the eleven sparse-baseline medians are class 1 (STaRK-Amazon, STaRK-MAG, STaRK-Prime, MetaQA, WebQSP) and four are class 2 (MuSiQue, HotpotQA, MultiHop-RAG, 2WikiMultiHop). TriviaQA is class 4. Natural Questions is mixed: two class-2 pairs and one class-4 pair, and its median of 4.8 is the class-4 pair. The dense-baseline GraphRAG-Bench median is class 2.

Pooled, class 1 gains exceed class 2 gains (23.7 vs 18.9). That gap comes from confounding: every class-1 pair sits at C_KG ≥ 0.75 and every C_KG = 0 pair is class 2 or 4. Among C_KG ≥ 0.75 benchmarks, class 1 and class 2 have the same median (23.7 vs 23.4, n = 11 vs 5). Within class 2, gains track C_KG (23.4 at C_KG ≥ 0.75 vs 7.1 at C_KG = 0). So the published record shows no larger gains for systems that rank over the gold's source structure than for self-built graphs on the same kind of benchmark. Class 3 cannot be compared because it has no pairs.

Class 1 is also confounded in other ways. All eleven class-1 pairs use answer or entity Hits@1, and seven are KBQA "KB vs text-corpus" contrasts from 2018–2019 (PullNet Tables 2 and 5), not passage retrieval against a lexical retriever over the same passages.

**What this means for B.11's closing sentence.** None of the 30 pairs uses a question-induced structure, which is the swap's maximal aligned arm. The high-C_KG gains in the published record come from two sources:

- **Class 1 (full gold-source KBs).** These are KBQA or STaRK settings, and they correspond to the paper's "realistic full KB" condition. There, on 2WikiMultiHop (live Wikidata KG) and MetaQA (full KB), the paper's controls inflate only pure PPR, and only modestly; STaRK-Prime's KB over the whole corpus also inflates the hybrid-PPR retriever in one held-out cell.
- **Class 2 (self-built OpenIE graphs on 2WikiMultiHop and GraphRAG-Bench).** Here the manuscript's own partial-alignment evidence applies (line ~754: the OpenIE KG contains 73.0% and 80.9% of the silver inventory's node surface forms). HippoRAG attributes its 2Wiki gain to "2WikiMultiHopQA's entity-centric design, which is particularly well-suited for HippoRAG" (arXiv 2405.14831, p.6, §4).

A same-system check points the same way. HippoRAG 2's increment over its own dense backbone (NV-Embed-v2, HippoRAG 2 Table 3) is +13.9 on 2Wiki, +5.0 on MuSiQue, +1.8 on HotpotQA, and +2.6 on NQ. That ordering follows C_KG even though the structure is class 2.

The sentence "the construction-time condition measured by the census co-occurs in the published record with the advantage pattern isolated by the swap" is therefore accurate as a co-occurrence. It should not be read as the swap's aligned mechanism operating in published systems. Stating the class composition in one clause would pre-empt that reading, for example: "no published pair ranks over a question-induced structure; high-C_KG gains come from full gold-source KBs (11 pairs) or self-built graphs (5 pairs)".

## Published systems that rank over the gold's source structure (class 1)

1. **PullNet and GraftNet on MetaQA and WebQSP** (Sun et al. 2019, EMNLP, p.5 §4.1, quoted above; Sun et al. 2018, EMNLP). Both retrieve over the WikiMovies KB that generated MetaQA and over the Freebase that WebQSP parses execute against.
2. **Think-on-Graph** (Sun et al., ICLR 2024, arXiv 2307.07697). This is a published natural analogue of a full-KB swap. p.6, §3.1.3: "Freebase (Bollacker et al., 2008) is used as KG for CWQ, WebQSP, GrailQA, Simple Questions, and Webquestions, and Wikidata [...] is used as KG for QALD10-en, T-REx, Zero-Shot RE and Creak." Table 3 (p.7; 1,000-question subsets, Hits@1) swaps the KG:
   - WebQSP: ToG 76.2 with Freebase vs 68.6 with Wikidata; ToG-R 75.1 vs 66.7.
   - CWQ: ToG 58.8 vs 54.9; ToG-R 59.2 vs 51.9.

   The authors explain (p.8, §3.2.3): "Notably, Freebase brings more significant improvements on CWQ and WebQSP than Wikidata, since both datasets are constructed upon Freebase." They add a coverage confound: "in a very large KG like Wikidata, the searching and pruning processes are relatively challenging."
3. **MoR over the STaRK SKBs** (Lei et al., Findings ACL 2025). Quote above.
4. **Hyperlink-graph retrieval on HotpotQA** (Asai et al., ICLR 2020, arXiv 1911.10470). p.3, §3.1: "We use the hyperlinks to construct the direct edges in G." HotpotQA's paragraph pairs were themselves sampled from hyperlink edges (Yang et al. 2018, p.2, §2): "we build a directed graph G, where each edge (a, b) indicates there is a hyperlink from the first paragraph of article a to article b".

   Caveat for the census: HotpotQA is scored C_KG = 0 on its sentence and answer axes. One could argue that its passage pairs were selected under hyperlink-graph guidance, which is rubric bin 1 on a passage axis the census does not score. This does not change the flag, because bin 1 is below the derived threshold, but it is worth one footnote.

## Published evaluations that ingest a benchmark's released evidence structure (class 3)

No published system or evaluation was found that uses 2WikiMultiHop's released `evidences` triples or GraphRAG-Bench's `evidence_relations` as its retrieval graph. The search covered web search over arXiv/ACL/OpenReview plus the GraphRAG-Bench and HippoRAG-family papers, and was not exhaustive.

The closest cases are oracle or upper-bound conditions, or benchmark-official subgraphs:

- **GraftNet** (Sun et al. 2018, p.8, §5.4): "In an oracle setting where we ensure the answers are part of the subgraph, the F1 score increases by 4.8%." The retrieval subgraph is augmented with gold answers on WebQSP.
- **KGQAGen** (Zhang et al., NeurIPS 2025 D&B, p.9, §6): the LLM-SP condition models are "directly given the associated supporting subgraph of the questions from the dataset construction process. These setups simulate perfect retrieval". The result: "GPT-4o (w/ SP) sees its LASM accuracy rise from 54.2% to 84.9% compared with GPT-4o."
- **Cattaneo et al.** (TMLR 09/2026, arXiv 2511.04473v3, App. C.1, p.30), the closest to the paper's mechanism. The official GTSQA question-specific graphs re-add pruned ground-truth edges: "If any of the edges in the (full) ground-truth answer subgraph of the question have been dropped as a result of pruning, they are re-added to the graph to guarantee that perfect retrieval is still possible. However, we observe empirically that this final step can unfairly bias retrievers towards the ground-truth edges that have been re-added." They counter this with same-metapath confounder edges. On the widely reused WebQSP/CWQ question-specific graphs of Luo et al. (2024), they write that "the selection of these starting graphs can strongly impact the final performance statistics, potentially over-representing [...] the retriever's capabilities" and that "this crucial detail on experimental setup is often not reported in papers".
- **MeLLo** (Zhong et al., EMNLP 2023, MQuAKE). It retrieves from a memory of the benchmark's own edited facts, by task design.

Quotes are from the PDFs in `pdfs/`, and the per-class statistics come from `class_stats.py`.
