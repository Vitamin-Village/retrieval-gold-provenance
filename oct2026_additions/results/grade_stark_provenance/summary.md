## Rubric mapping (manuscript App. A.1)

Rubric (verbatim): bin 4 (1.0): gold copied directly from the evaluation KG, or selected solely because it contains the answer string; bin 3 (0.75): auto-generated from KG triples or templates, or evidence retained by answer-string distant supervision; bin 2 (0.50): gold derived from K or A by auto-generation and then human-validated; bin 1 (0.25): human or text gold with KG-guided or answer-guided selection; bin 0: independent of K and A. Step 3: KG augmentation, human refinement, and anti-leakage filtering each shift the bin one step lower (at most twice).

### GRADE edge axis (census bin 3)
- Order: articles -> fact sentences -> LLM claims -> LLM triples -> KG (DiGraph, code/graph/find_DAG_path_shortest.py:15), augmented by mirrored edges that copy the source row (code/clustering/add_contextually_same_path.py:64, flag code/clustering/add_contextually_same_path.py:67); gold edge set P = a shortest path whose every triple is read from a graph edge (code/graph/find_DAG_path_shortest.py:30); 2-5 hops, one path per endpoint pair, 400 per hop (code/sampling/sample_path.py:9; code/sampling/sample_path.py:13; code/sampling/sample_path.py:21).
- Base bin 4 (copied from the construction KG by code); Step 3 KG augmentation -1 -> bin 3. No human refinement: the only post-generation filter is an o1-mini ambiguity check (code/qa/final_validation.py:50,62); the paper's human check audits 30 entity groups (App. A.6) and its Limitations list human-in-the-loop verification as future work. Census bin 3 stands.
