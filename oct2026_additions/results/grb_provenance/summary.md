## Construction record (primary sources, verbatim)

- arXiv 2506.05690v3 (ICLR 2026 version), App. A.3, p.18: We begin by extracting information from corpora to build ontologies or knowledge graphs. Taking the Novel dataset as an example, we first construct a knowledge graph from the corpus, then form logic chains using relevant triples from this graph. Then, we control difficulty by adjusting the number of involved triples and the information span they cover.
- arXiv 2506.05690v3, App. C.2 Logic Mining, p.20: we transform text into formalized ontologies using GPT-4.1 [...] This ontology acts as a ground-truth map of domain logic
- arXiv 2506.05690v3, App. C.4 Question Generation, p.21: Questions are generated by aligning their cognitive demands with the structural properties of the underlying evidence. Retrieval-focused questions target isolated subgraphs
- arXiv 2506.05690v3, App. C.5 Check & Correct, pp.21-22: given the original corpus and the constructed evidence, we assess whether the evidence can be logically derived from the corpus. Our validation criteria are strict: given a question, if no evidence triple can be inferred from the corpus, the corresponding question is discarded. Similarly, for answer correction, we check whether the provided answer can be logically inferred from the evidence. This verification and correction process is supported by advanced models combined with human checking for final confirmation.
- arXiv 2506.05690v3, App. C.6 Refinement, p.23: for each question, we locate the corpus segment from which the evidence was derived and employ GPT-4.1 to refine the original question by integrating this segment.
- arXiv 2506.05690v3, Fig. 10 (Medical construction prompts), p.32: Fact Retrieval: 'Create information-seeking questions that require direct factual retrieval from the relations list.' Contextual Summarize: 'should ideally cover **the majority of the ontology**' / 'Do NOT infer or invent unstated details or use any information not explicitly stated in the input or ontology.'
